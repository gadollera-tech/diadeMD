ABOUT MEDUDEMY

An ongoing med-school vault built as I learn.

MeduDemy is where I organize the reviewers, questions, references, files, and study systems I create while going through medical school. It is intentionally a living project—not a finished review center or complete medical database.

From DiadeMD to MeduDemy: DiadeMD was the original working name for this personal medical-learning project. It evolved into MeduDemy so it could sit under the GDtal Labs family alongside TutoDemy while keeping its original “built while learning medicine” spirit.

WHAT IT IS RIGHT NOW

Part study vault. Part diary. Part learning lab.

I am building it alongside my own medical education, so the content grows with the subjects and systems I am actually studying. Some sections are complete enough to use now; others are intentionally marked as future spaces.

The long-term vision can expand from NMAT to med school and eventually PLE review, but the current priority is building a trustworthy, organized medical-school knowledge vault one topic at a time.

PUBLISHING STANDARDS

Medical content needs a higher verification bar.

01

Reliable references

Use standard texts, official guidance, and appropriate peer-reviewed sources.

02

Label simplifications

Distinguish summaries, mnemonics, approximations, and varying recommendations.

03

Review critical details

Verify doses, thresholds, algorithms, and emergency steps.

04

Show versions

Display author, reviewer, date, and correction history.

05

Protect privacy

De-identify cases and reject patient-identifying uploads.

06

Invite correction

Provide a transparent error-reporting workflow.
