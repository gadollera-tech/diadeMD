ABOUT DIADEMD

A medical learning project built from the inside.

DiadeMD is being developed during the medical-school journey—turning scattered notes, questions, cases, and difficult concepts into structured learning tools.

Founder • Medical Student • Learning Designer

THE VISION

From admission preparation to clinical thinking.

Students often use separate tools at every stage—NMAT reviewers, lecture notes, question banks, flashcards, and clinical references. DiadeMD aims to connect these stages through a consistent study workflow.

The platform can grow in phases: verified static resources, interactive questions, then accounts, progress tracking, and contributor review.

PUBLISHING STANDARDS

Medical content deserves a higher verification bar.

01

Use reliable references

Base content on standard texts, official guidance, and current primary or institutional sources as appropriate.

02

Separate fact from simplification

Label mnemonics, approximations, local practice notes, and areas of disagreement.

03

Review clinically important details

Verify doses, contraindications, thresholds, algorithms, and emergency steps.

04

Show review dates

State when a resource was written, checked, updated, or corrected.

05

Protect privacy

De-identify cases and obtain permission before using real clinical material.

06

Invite correction

Provide a transparent process for reporting errors and updating materials.

CONTRIBUTE

Build a reviewer, verify a question, or improve an explanation.

The future workflow can include author, medical reviewer, editor, and design roles—with visible version history.
