# MeduDemy Brand Kit

## Brand
**MeduDemy** is a medical learning platform under **GDtal Labs**.

**Positioning:** A structured learning companion from NMAT preparation through medical school and future PLE review.

**Primary tagline:**  
**Learn. Review. Become.**

## Brand architecture
- **GDtal Labs** — parent / umbrella brand
- **TutoDemy** — tutoring, academic help, CET and scholarship-exam preparation
- **MeduDemy** — NMAT, medical school, PLE, reviewers, QBank, and study tools
- **DiadeMD** — former working name / origin of the MeduDemy medical-learning project

## Logo usage
- Keep clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch or compress it.
- Do not recolor individual letters outside the approved teal-magenta system.
- Do not place the standard dark logo on similarly dark backgrounds without enough contrast.
- Prefer the simplified icon or wordmark at very small sizes.
- Avoid adding drop shadows, extra outlines, or unrelated medical symbols.

## Voice
MeduDemy should sound:
- Clear
- Helpful
- Student-centered
- Evidence-conscious
- Encouraging without being childish
- Practical and high-yield

Avoid:
- Overclaiming exam outcomes
- Presenting educational content as medical advice
- Publishing unverified recalled exam questions
- Making the platform sound like an official school or licensing-body resource
