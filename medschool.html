ORGAN SYSTEMS

Build from foundations to systems.

♥ Cardiovascular +

Anatomy → electrophysiology → hemodynamics → pathology → pharmacology → ECG and cases.

◌ Respiratory +

Airway and lungs → ventilation/perfusion → gas exchange → pathology → imaging and support.

⚕ Renal and urinary +

Nephron → filtration and transport → fluids/electrolytes → acid–base → kidney disease.

⌁ Gastrointestinal and hepatobiliary +

Motility and digestion → absorption → liver/pancreas → inflammation, obstruction, bleeding.

✦ Nervous system +

Neuroanatomy → signaling → pathways → localization → neurologic syndromes.

◎ Endocrine and reproductive +

Hormone axes → metabolism → reproductive physiology → disorders.
