ORGAN SYSTEMS

Integrate horizontally and vertically.

Start with normal structure and function, then connect pathology, diagnostics, treatment, and cases.

♥Cardiovascular system+

Anatomy and circulation → electrophysiology → hemodynamics → vascular and cardiac pathology → pharmacology → ECG and cases.

View planned resources →
◌Respiratory system+

Airway and lung anatomy → ventilation and perfusion → gas exchange → obstructive and restrictive disease → imaging and management.

⚕Renal and urinary system+

Nephron structure → filtration and transport → fluids and electrolytes → acid-base → AKI, CKD, and urinary disease.

⌁Gastrointestinal system+

GI anatomy and motility → digestion and absorption → liver and pancreas → inflammation, obstruction, infection, and neoplasia.

✦Nervous system+

Neuroanatomy → signaling → sensory and motor pathways → localization → neurologic syndromes and emergencies.

◎Endocrine and reproductive systems+

Hormone regulation → metabolism → reproductive physiology → endocrine and reproductive disorders.
