CURRENT TEST FRAMEWORK

Two parts, eight core subtests.

This page follows the official CEM description available during development. Reconfirm details before each review cycle.

PART 1

Mental Ability

Reasoning, speed, pattern recognition, and careful interpretation.

VerbalAnalogies and reading comprehension
Inductive ReasoningSeries, figures, and relationships
QuantitativeComputation, problems, and data
Perceptual AcuityVisual recognition and accuracy
PART 2

Academic Proficiency

BiologyCore life-science concepts

ChemistryGeneral and foundational chemistry
Official timing snapshot: CEM currently describes 120 items in Part 1 and 120 items in Part 2 with section-specific time limits. Review the latest official bulletin and candidate guide rather than relying on memory.
HOW TO STUDY

Train differently for Part 1 and Part 2.

Part 1 improves through repeated exposure, strategy refinement, and timed accuracy. Part 2 improves through concept review, application, and mixed practice.

Part 1 approach

  • Short, frequent timed drills
  • Pattern and error classification
  • Speed without careless guessing
  • Visual and reading stamina

Part 2 approach

  • Topic diagnostics
  • Focused concept rebuilding
  • Equation and data practice
  • Mixed-subject retrieval
12-WEEK EXAMPLE ROADMAP

A balanced preparation cycle.

Adjust based on baseline performance, school workload, and the official test date.

Weeks 1–2Diagnose and organize

Baseline sets, resources, error categories, and a realistic schedule.

Weeks 3–6Rebuild weak foundations

Systematic academic review plus daily mental-ability practice.

Weeks 7–9Integrate and time

Mixed sets, pacing drills, and longer simulations.

Weeks 10–11Simulate and analyze

Full-length practice, error review, and priority refreshers.

Week 12Taper and prepare

Light recall, logistics, sleep protection, and confidence routines.
