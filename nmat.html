PART 1 TRAINING

Short, frequent, timed.

  • Classify recurring errors.
  • Track accuracy separately from speed.
  • Practice skip-return decisions.
  • Build reading and visual stamina.
PART 2 TRAINING

Diagnose, rebuild, mix.

  • Begin with a topic diagnostic.
  • Relearn weak concepts before hard drills.
  • Mix quantitative and conceptual questions.
  • Use error logs to choose the next review block.
